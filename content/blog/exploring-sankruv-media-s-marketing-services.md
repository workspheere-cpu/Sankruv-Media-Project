Sankruv Media is a digital marketing agency in Mumbai that helps brands build a strong digital identity and grow online. Over more than five years we have worked with over 50 clients and delivered more than 700 campaigns across lifestyle, fashion, retail, corporate and real estate.

Here is an overview of what we do and how our services work together.

## One team, four services

### Social Media Management

We plan, create and manage your social media across Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, YouTube and more. That includes content strategy, posts, reels, stories, copywriting, influencer collaborations and monthly analytics reports. [Explore social media management]({{root}}social-media-management.html).

### Web Development & Design

We design and build responsive, high-performing websites, from portfolio and business sites to eCommerce stores on Shopify, WooCommerce or custom platforms, with copywriting, landing pages, speed optimisation and maintenance. [Explore web development and design]({{root}}web-development-design.html).

### Branding & Creative Design

We create visual identities that tell your story: logos, brand guidelines, packaging, print collateral, social media themes, ad creatives and pitch decks. [Explore branding and creative design]({{root}}branding-creative-design.html).

### Corporate & Commercial Shoots

We produce photo and video for products, corporate profiles, promotions, events, reels, ad films and lifestyle and fashion shoots, and edit it for every channel. [Explore corporate and commercial shoots]({{root}}corporate-commercial-shoots.html).

## Why an end-to-end team helps

- **Consistency.** Your social media, website, brand and visuals come from one team, so they look and sound like one brand.
- **One point of contact.** No need to manage and brief several vendors.
- **Faster delivery.** Strategy, design, production and publishing happen under one roof.

## How we work

1. **Discover.** We learn your brand, audience and goals.
2. **Plan.** We build a strategy and a monthly content plan grounded in data.
3. **Create.** Our team produces the design, content, shoots and development.
4. **Launch and report.** We go live, track results and share monthly insights.

Customers now research almost everything online before they buy. They search, compare, read reviews, check social media pages and visit websites before they ever speak to a business. If your brand is not visible and convincing online, many potential customers will choose someone who is.

Here is why internet marketing has become essential, and where to begin.

## Your customers are already online

Whether you sell fashion, furniture, real estate or professional services, your customers use their phones to discover and evaluate brands. Internet marketing puts your business in front of them at the moment they are looking.

## Reach the right people, not just more people

Online channels let you reach people by location, interests, age and behaviour. Instead of paying to reach everyone, you can focus your budget on the audience most likely to become customers.

## Measure what works

Unlike many traditional channels, online marketing shows you what happens after people see your message. You can track visits, clicks, enquiries and sales, then put more effort into the campaigns that perform.

## Compete with bigger brands

A well-designed website, consistent social media and smart campaigns can make a growing business look as credible as an established one. Quality and consistency matter more than size.

## Build trust before the first conversation

People trust brands that look professional and show proof. Your website, social media content, customer stories and reviews do the work of building confidence before a customer contacts you.

## The core channels to consider

- **Website.** Your home online and the place where interest turns into enquiries or sales.
- **Social media.** Where people discover brands, follow them and engage with content.
- **Search.** Where people go when they are actively looking for what you offer.
- **Paid advertising.** A faster way to reach a defined audience with a specific offer.
- **Direct messaging and email.** Channels that keep you in touch with interested customers.

## Where to start

1. **Get the foundations right.** A clear brand, a professional website and active social profiles.
2. **Choose your priority channels.** Focus on the two or three your customers use most.
3. **Publish consistently and measure.** Follow a monthly plan and review the results.

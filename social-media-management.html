Social Media Management

Social media management in Mumbai

Content creation, targeted strategy and brand storytelling across every major platform, planned monthly and measured every month.

Get a free quote WhatsApp us

Social media is often the first place a customer meets your brand. We plan, create and manage your presence across Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, YouTube and the other platforms your audience uses, so every post has a purpose and your brand looks and sounds the same everywhere.

Each month starts with a content plan built around your goals and ends with a report that shows what worked. In between, our team handles the writing, design and reels, so you can focus on running the business.

Best for

Brands that want a consistent, professional social presence without building an in-house content team.

What is included

6 services, planned together and delivered by one team. Choose the full set or the parts you need.

  1. 01

    Content strategy and monthly planning

    We define your content pillars, posting rhythm and campaign moments, then map them into a monthly calendar before production begins.

  2. 02

    Posts, reels, stories and copywriting

    Designed posts, short-form video, stories and captions written in your brand voice, produced by one team so everything feels connected.

  3. 03

    Account management

    Day-to-day management of Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, YouTube and more, keeping every channel active, current and consistent.

  4. 04

    Influencer collaborations

    We identify creators who suit your brand, coordinate the collaboration and align their content with your campaign.

  5. 05

    Growth and engagement strategy

    Formats, timing and tactics guided by what your audience responds to, so reach and engagement grow over time.

  6. 06

    Monthly analytics and insight reports

    A clear report on reach, engagement and growth, with the insights that shape the next month's plan.

What you get

  • A content calendar planned a month ahead
  • One recognisable look and voice on every platform
  • Monthly numbers and insights instead of guesswork

How it works

Four clear steps from brief to results.

  1. 01

    Audit

    We review your current profiles, audience and competitors to find what to keep, fix and build on.

  2. 02

    Plan

    We set content pillars and goals, then build a monthly calendar of posts, reels, stories and campaigns.

  3. 03

    Create and publish

    Our team designs, shoots, writes and schedules the content across your channels.

  4. 04

    Report

    Every month you receive analytics and insights, and the next plan improves on what performed best.
